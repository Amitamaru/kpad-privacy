Data and permissions

KPAD Messages acts as your default SMS app with your consent. SMS/MMS content, numbers, timestamps and status are used for receiving, sending, history, search, read state and notifications. Optional contacts permission provides names and recipient selection; you can enter a number manually instead. SIM information supports SIM selection. Android controls permissions and the SMS role; restricted SMS access stops when the role is lost.

Permissions used by the app

Access is controlled through the system SMS role and the corresponding Android permissions. Contacts and SIM information are requested in the context of the relevant feature; notification permission is requested on Android versions that require it.

SMS/MMS
Read, receive and send messages when the SMS role and corresponding permissions are granted.
Contacts — optional
Read names, numbers, starred contacts and groups for search and recipient selection. You can enter a number manually; the app has no permission to modify contacts.
Phone / SIM information — optional
List active SIMs and select a SIM for sending.
Notifications and vibration
Notify you about messages within Android permissions and settings.
Android boot completion
Resume pending local messaging work after a phone restart.

Attachments and backup documents are selected through Android’s system document picker — the Storage Access Framework (SAF). The app does not request broad access to all files. Taking a photo opens a separate camera app at your request. KPAD does not request its own camera, microphone or location permissions.

Local storage

History remains in the Android system database. Drafts, templates, settings, bounded conversation previews, attachments and operation records are stored privately by the app. There are no developer accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs or developer servers. Technical logs must not contain message text or phone numbers.

Data transfers

Sending SMS/MMS transfers data through Android and your carrier to your chosen recipients. SMS is not end-to-end encrypted. MMS depends on carrier support and has not completed network acceptance testing. Your system keyboard is a separate app with its own policy; KPAD does not forcibly change it. Clipboard, attachment viewers and document providers receive data only through your explicit actions.

Backups and export

Create and verify backup creates an unencrypted format-2 ZIP in the private message-backups folder. It contains SMS/MMS, attachments and selected local state. It survives closing the app and reboot, but uninstall deletes it. Earlier archives are not automatically removed. Save backup to file exports the existing archive through Android to your chosen destination. That independent file may survive uninstall. A cloud document provider may transfer it under its own terms; there is no automatic developer-server upload.

Important: the ZIP is not encrypted. Anyone or any service with access to the archive can read its messages, numbers and attachments.

If export fails, the original private archive is preserved. The document provider may leave an incomplete file at the selected destination; check it and remove it yourself if needed.

Restore and integrity

SHA-256 verifies archive integrity; it is not encryption or proof of successful recovery. Choosing a backup creates a private import for validation and preview. Restore requires a separate confirmation and the SMS role, and adds data with duplicate checks. Recovery against a real system database has not completed acceptance testing. Keep an independent backup of important messages and protect exported ZIP files.

Control and deletion

Android automatic backup is disabled. Android12+ rules exclude app data from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer; explicit export remains available. You can change permissions and the SMS role, delete selected messages after confirmation, and delete exported files yourself. SMS deletion may be irreversible. The app cannot control external copies. No data is retained on a developer server; private local data remains until deletion or uninstall. For privacy questions, contact Dmytro Marzhiievskyi at dmytromarzhiievskyi@gmail.com.

Notifications and retained imports

Sound, vibration and notification privacy preferences are stored locally. Android and notification channels take priority. Local sound/vibration switches do not read SMS or transmit data. Hiding content keeps the contact name or sender number in the unlocked notification shade but removes SMS text from notifications, including expanded views. Android may additionally hide the sender on the lock screen. Privacy changes silently update only active notifications, reading their conversations without republishing history or changing SMS. The bounded cache may be refreshed or cleared according to permissions and limits; it is not a backup. Selected imported archives, including incomplete or invalid copies, may remain in private storage after validation errors. System SMS/MMS history and app-private data are separate; uninstalling the app does not guarantee deletion of system history.
